Bediz (previously SkyImage and MyImage) 3.1.0 — build 127
Microsoft Store package: 3.1.4.0

- Recents previews stay visible after capture; only the new capture loads.
- Paste text onto an open canvas as an editable text box, keeping line breaks and your Text style. Paste images as movable, resizable layers. Text fields retain normal paste behavior.
- Completed Quick Fills keep their colors when you choose the next color or transparency. Select an existing fill to recolor it.
- Window scrolling captures independently scrolling panes in their original columns and preserves fixed headers, footers and sidebars.
- After selecting a Region or Window, choose Capture for a still image or the scroll icon for a longer image, then Done. Pause, restart or cancel; Escape cancels selection.
- Compact capture controls, editable hotkeys and a close button in Quick Start & Hotkeys.
- Output defaults to 100%; choose 75%, 50% or 25% for a smaller finished image.
- Existing MyImage and SkyImage projects, settings and Store updates carry forward under Bediz.
