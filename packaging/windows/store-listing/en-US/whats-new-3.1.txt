Bediz (previously SkyImage and MyImage) 3.1.0 — build 124
Microsoft Store package: 3.1.2.0

- SkyImage is now Bediz on Mac and Windows. Existing MyImage and SkyImage projects, settings and update identity carry forward.
- Window scrolling captures independently scrolling panes in their original columns. Scroll each pane down in turn, then choose Done to combine them.
- Combined captures preserve fixed headers and footers, with plain space below shorter panes and a pane count in the preview.
- Fix scrolling capture after selecting a window, preserving the window target and handling fixed toolbars and sidebars.
- Compact capture toolbar with icons, tooltips and a clear output percentage.
- Keep Region or Window as your selection mode. After selecting, choose Capture for a still image or the scroll icon to collect a longer image, then Done.
- Pause, restart or cancel scrolling capture. Escape cancels selection even when a capture menu is open.
- Scrolling capture ignores localized cursor changes so repeated sections are not duplicated.
- Output size still defaults to 100%; choose 75%, 50% or 25% for a smaller finished image.
- Click the shortcut in Capture Now to customize your capture hotkey.
- Quick Start & Hotkeys has a visible close button as well as Escape.
- The compact capture controls and automatic region scrolling are shared with Mac.
- Store-installed copies continue to check Microsoft Store for updates.
